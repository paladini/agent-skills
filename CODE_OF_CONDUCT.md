# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good intent, acknowledge sources, and focus criticism on ideas and artifacts rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
